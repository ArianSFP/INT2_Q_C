## Finding

The v0 runner fits alpha to 97% of each expert payload and then kills a cell if
any expert's ideal KL is above that same 97% threshold. The implementation did
exactly that. However, its status token is
`HARD_KILL_IDEAL_KL_EXCEEDS_FROZEN_RESERVOIR`, and its result field named
`reservoir_bits_by_expert` contains the full 100% physical payload. That wording
overstates what occurred: no expert exceeded the physical reservoir.

| QSB250 | 11,730,944 | 11,379,015.68 | 1, 2, 5 | 892.447966 bits | 351,035.872034 bits (2.992392%) |

Across all 18 expert/cell rows, physical margins are positive and range from
about 2.9806% to 3.0227% of the payload. The precise per-expert values are in
`recomputed_margins.json` and are independently regenerated by the verifier.

`POLICY_REJECT_ALL_RATE_CELLS` is therefore supportable only as the outcome of
the preregistered v0 97% safety policy. It is not evidence of physical bitstream
overflow. Because the early rate gate fired, no matrix decoder oracle, MSE,
capture, matched Gaussian control, or compression result was produced.

The result's own claim boundary remains correct: even a future oracle completion
would be optimistic and source-leaking, not a serialized channel simulator,
